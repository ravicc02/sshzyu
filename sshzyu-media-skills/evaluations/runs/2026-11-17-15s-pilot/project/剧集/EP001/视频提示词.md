# EP001 视频提示词

## MOTION-EP001-001 · 走廊尽头的背影

- 分镜：SHOT-EP001-001
- 时长：5s
- 生成方式：文生视频
- 输入参考图：无
- 开场画面：凌晨的写字楼十八层走廊，冷白灯管轻微闪烁；林晚背对镜头站在走廊中段，身穿深灰风衣，走廊尽头是闭合的不锈钢电梯门。
- 起始帧：林晚背对镜头站在走廊中段，右脚刚离地。
- 动作过程：沿走廊走向电梯 → 头顶灯管闪一下 → 在闭合的电梯门前站定
- 终点：林晚背对镜头停在电梯门前，双手垂在身侧。

### 可复制提示词
> 9:16 vertical, 5 seconds, realistic live-action cinema, cold cyan-teal color grade, shallow depth of field, fine film grain. A long empty office corridor on the 18th floor at night. Lin Wan, a 28-year-old office worker with black mid-length hair tied low, seen from behind in a charcoal-grey trench coat over dark trousers, starts walking away from camera along the corridor toward the elevator at the far end. Her coat hem sways with each step and her shoulders stay slightly hunched with fatigue. As she passes beneath the ceiling tubes, one cold-white fluorescent tube flickers once and briefly dims the corridor. She reaches the closed brushed stainless-steel elevator doors and stops, her hands coming to rest at her sides. Camera locked off at eye level, 35mm, the corridor's vanishing point and the elevator doors held centered. Ambient building hum and soft heel steps only, no dialogue, no lip-sync. The amber floor indicator above the doors stays at 18. End on her standing still before the closed doors.

## MOTION-EP001-002 · 空轿厢与镜面

- 分镜：SHOT-EP001-002
- 时长：5s
- 生成方式：文生视频
- 输入参考图：无
- 开场画面：同一走廊，林晚背对镜头站在闭合的电梯门前；门尚未打开，门上方楼层屏显示 18。
- 起始帧：电梯门闭合，林晚背对镜头站在门前，右手垂在身侧。
- 动作过程：门向两侧滑开 → 露出空轿厢 → 镜面墙映出她疲惫的脸，她缓慢眨一次眼
- 终点：门完全打开，空轿厢与镜中她的脸同时停在画面里。

### 可复制提示词
> 9:16 vertical, 5 seconds, realistic live-action cinema, cold cyan-teal color grade, shallow depth of field, fine film grain. In the same empty office corridor at night, Lin Wan stands facing the closed elevator doors, seen from behind and slightly to her left, in a charcoal-grey trench coat over dark trousers. The brushed stainless-steel doors slide open smoothly to both sides and reveal an empty car: mirrored walls, a vertical handrail, a cold-white ceiling panel, no one inside. As the gap widens, her reflection surfaces in the mirror wall — a tired narrow face, faint dark circles under the eyes, loose strands of black hair at her cheek, the coat collar at her shoulders; she blinks once, slowly. Camera fixed at a slightly low eye-level angle with a very slow, subtle push-in as the doors finish opening. Elevator arrival chime and the soft slide of the door track, no dialogue, no lip-sync. End with the doors fully open, the empty car and her reflected face held in the frame.

## MOTION-EP001-003 · 楼层跳到 B3

- 分镜：SHOT-EP001-003
- 时长：5s
- 生成方式：文生视频
- 输入参考图：无
- 开场画面：电梯轿厢内，林晚侧身站在门边，面向按钮面板，右手垂在身侧；门上方楼层屏显示 18。
- 起始帧：林晚跨过门槛站在轿厢内，侧身面向按钮面板，右手垂在身侧。
- 动作过程：抬起右手，指尖伸向开门键 → 楼层屏由 18 跳到 B3 → 指尖停在按钮前，她抬眼看向楼层屏
- 终点：指尖悬在开门键前没有落下，楼层屏停在 B3。

### 可复制提示词
> 9:16 vertical, 5 seconds, realistic live-action cinema, cold cyan-teal color grade, shallow depth of field, fine film grain. Inside a mirrored elevator car at night, Lin Wan steps across the threshold and turns her shoulder toward the vertical brushed-steel button panel beside the door, seen in three-quarter profile, black mid-length hair tied low, a charcoal-grey trench coat over a black high-neck knit, tired face lit by cold-white ceiling light. She raises her right hand to chest height and extends her index finger toward the open-door button at the bottom of the panel. Before she touches it, the amber LED floor indicator above the doors flips from 18 to B3 with a soft click. Her finger stops in mid-air just short of the button; her eyes lift toward the indicator and her breathing pauses. Camera fixed at eye level, 35mm, focus easing from her profile to the button panel and the indicator. Low elevator hum and one soft relay click from the indicator, no dialogue, no lip-sync. End on her fingertip hovering before the button and the indicator holding B3.
